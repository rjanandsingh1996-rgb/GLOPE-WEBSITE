Cancellation Policy

A simple process for cancelling a direct GLOPE order.

Effective / Last updated: 7 October 2026

1. Requesting cancellation

If you need to cancel an order, contact GLOPE as soon as possible using the phone or WhatsApp number provided during ordering. Include your name and order details so we can identify the request.

2. Before dispatch

Cancellation requests received before dispatch will generally be considered, subject to confirmation by GLOPE. If payment has already been received, any applicable refund will be processed after the cancellation is accepted.

3. After dispatch

Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible. If the delivered product has leakage or an eligible defect, please use the Returns & Refunds Policy.
