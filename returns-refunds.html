Returns & Refunds

A food-product-friendly process for genuine delivery, packaging and order issues.

Effective / Last updated: 7 October 2026

1. Food-product returns

Because GLOPE products are packaged food products, routine returns for change of mind after delivery are generally not accepted.

If a product is delivered with leakage, a material packaging defect, or another verified product/fulfilment defect, After the issue is verified from the proof provided, GLOPE will provide a full refund for the affected product/order.

2. Eligible issues

Please contact us if you receive:

  • A leaking or materially damaged package.
  • A product with a material packaging defect.
  • The wrong product or pack size.
  • Another verified fulfilment issue that makes the delivered order materially different from the order confirmed by GLOPE.

3. How to raise a request

Contact GLOPE as soon as reasonably possible after delivery, preferably within 48 hours, and provide your order details, name, phone number and clear photographs/video of the parcel and product.

For leakage or visible packaging/product defects, the proof should clearly show the condition of the parcel and product. Do not discard the product or packaging until GLOPE advises you on the next step.

After reviewing the proof, GLOPE will confirm the verified issue and process the applicable full refund.

4. Refunds

Where a leakage or defect is verified and a refund is approved, GLOPE will provide a full refund for the affected order/product as applicable.

The full refund will be processed using the appropriate payment method or method communicated by GLOPE. The time taken for the amount to reflect may depend on the payment method or financial institution.

This policy does not remove any consumer right or remedy available under applicable law.
