Returns & Refunds

A food-product-friendly process for genuine delivery, packaging and order issues.

Effective / Last updated: 7 October 2026

1. Food-product returns

Because GLOPE products include packaged food, we generally do not accept routine returns for change of mind after delivery or for products that have been opened or used, except where a return, replacement or other remedy is required by applicable law.

2. When you should contact us

Please contact us promptly if you receive:

  • The wrong product or pack size.
  • A damaged or leaking package.
  • A product that appears tampered with or has a material packaging/fulfilment issue.
  • Another issue that makes the delivered order materially different from the order confirmed by GLOPE.

3. How to raise a request

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery where possible and provide your order details, name, phone number and clear photographs/video of the parcel and product. Do not discard the product or packaging until we advise you on the next step.

We may ask for additional information to verify the issue. Depending on the circumstances, we may offer a replacement, refund or another appropriate remedy.

4. Refunds

Where a refund is approved, we will communicate the refund method and expected processing timeline. The timing of a credit may also depend on the payment method or financial institution used.

No refund policy is intended to remove a consumer's rights or remedies available under applicable law.
