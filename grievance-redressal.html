How to raise a grievance

  1. Contact the grievance officer using the details above.
  2. Include your name, order/reference details where relevant, contact number and a clear description of the issue.
  3. For product or delivery complaints, include photographs or other evidence where available.
  4. We will acknowledge and review the grievance and communicate the next steps or resolution.
We aim to acknowledge consumer grievances within 48 hours and, where applicable, redress them within one month or within the period prescribed by applicable law.

Food safety concerns

If your concern relates to food safety or a suspected regulatory violation, you may also use the official FSSAI consumer grievance mechanism. GLOPE will cooperate with lawful regulatory requirements.
