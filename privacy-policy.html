Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of what information GLOPE may receive when you visit our website, contact us or place an order.

Effective / Last updated: 7 October 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the GLOPE website and to information shared with GLOPE in connection with product enquiries, orders, customer support and complaints.

GLOPE currently uses a direct-order model in which customers may contact us through WhatsApp, phone or email. We do not operate a customer account or website checkout on this site.

2. Information we may receive

Depending on how you interact with us, we may receive:

  • Name and contact details such as mobile number or email address.
  • Delivery address and pincode when you request an order.
  • Product, quantity, order and support information.
  • Information you voluntarily provide in WhatsApp, email or other communications.
  • Basic technical information generated when the website is accessed, such as browser/device information and security or hosting logs.
  • Your language preference on this website, which may be stored locally in your browser.

3. How we use information

We may use information to respond to enquiries, confirm and fulfil orders, arrange delivery, provide customer support, handle complaints, maintain business records, prevent misuse and comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

We do not use customer information for unrelated purposes merely because it has been provided to us.

4. Sharing and service providers

We may share only the information reasonably necessary with service providers involved in fulfilling your request, such as delivery/logistics partners or payment providers where applicable.

If you choose to contact GLOPE through WhatsApp, your communication is also processed by WhatsApp/Meta under their own terms and privacy practices. Instagram and other external websites linked from this website likewise operate under their own policies.

We may disclose information where required by law, legal process, regulatory authority or to protect the rights, safety and security of GLOPE, customers or others.

5. Data security and retention

We take reasonable organisational and technical measures appropriate to the nature of the information we handle. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, customer support, business records, dispute resolution, legal obligations or legitimate security needs.

6. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, or raise a privacy-related concern. Where applicable law provides additional rights, we will handle those requests in accordance with the applicable framework and its commencement dates.
