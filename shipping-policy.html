Shipping Policy

How GLOPE handles delivery information, dispatch, delivery issues and damaged parcels.

Effective / Last updated: 7 October 2026

1. Order confirmation

GLOPE currently accepts direct orders through communication channels such as WhatsApp. Before an order is accepted, we confirm product availability, final price, delivery address, pincode and any applicable delivery charges.

2. Delivery timeline

The expected dispatch and delivery timeline will be communicated at the time of order confirmation and may vary by destination, courier availability, weather, public holidays and other circumstances outside our reasonable control.

Customers should provide a complete and accurate address and remain reachable on the contact number shared for the order.

3. Shipping charges

Any applicable shipping or delivery charge will be communicated before the order is confirmed. We do not treat an order as confirmed without communicating the final amount payable.

4. Delivery issues

If a parcel is visibly damaged, leaking or appears tampered with at delivery, please photograph the package and contact GLOPE as soon as reasonably possible. Where practical, customers should avoid accepting a severely damaged parcel and should immediately inform the delivery provider.

If a parcel is delivered to the wrong address because the customer supplied incorrect information, additional delivery arrangements may be required.

5. Product safety

GLOPE supplies packaged food products. Customers should check the physical label, storage instructions and product condition after delivery and store the product as instructed.

For a damaged, leaking, incorrect or otherwise problematic order, please see our Returns & Refunds Policy.
